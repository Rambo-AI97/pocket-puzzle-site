Pixel Pulse: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21 (v2)

Pixel Pulse is a Glyph Toy for the back display (Glyph Matrix) of the Nothing Phone (3) and Phone (4a) Pro: a retro clock with a north arrow, box breathing, and music bars. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information. Short version: nothing leaves your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

Microphone permission

Pixel Pulse asks for the microphone permission only because Android requires it to read the sound your phone itself is playing (Android's Visualizer). The app uses it to measure how loud the bass, middle and treble of that music are, so the bars on the back display can move with the beat. It never listens to the room, never records, and never saves or sends any audio. The numbers are used for the next frame of the bars and then thrown away. You can say no; the bars then show a gentle pulse instead.

Motion and compass sensors

The north arrow uses the phone's rotation sensor while the toy is on screen. The readings stay on the phone and are not saved.

Network

The app makes no network connections. Links on its settings screen (tip page, GitHub, email, Pocket Puzzle) simply open your browser or mail app when you tap them.

What is saved, and where

Your settings (12/24-hour clock, compass on/off, breathing pattern and similar) are saved only on your own device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes them.

Ads and purchases

There are no ads and nothing to buy inside the app. If a tip link is shown, it opens an outside tip page in your browser; that page has its own privacy policy and the app never sees anything about it.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date.
