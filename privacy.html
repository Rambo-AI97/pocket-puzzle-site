Pocket Puzzle: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

Pocket Puzzle is a small collection of offline puzzle games. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information. Short version: nothing leaves your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

Network

The app makes no network connections while you play. It works without internet.

What is saved, and where

Your game progress, settings, and stats (for example, games played and best scores) are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes them.

Ads and purchases

There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Sharing

We do not share data with third parties because we do not have any data. If you tap a Share button in the app, your phone's own share menu opens with a short text you can send; that is your choice and is handled by your phone, not by the app.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app.

Contact

Questions? Write to pocketpuzzle.app@gmail.com.
